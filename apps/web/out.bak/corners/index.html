  • Overs are priced sensibly at 8.5 or 9.5; over 10.5 lands only 38% of the time.
  • Unders belong at 11.5 or 12.5. Line 10 is the median, so it carries no edge either way.
  • At 60 minutes: if the favourite leads, unders are the side (21% of those matches went over 10.5); if it is level or behind, overs (62% went over 9.5).

How to read the pace

  • Projected is current corners divided by minutes played, times 90. It is an extrapolation, not a prediction.
  • Needed rate is the corners still required to clear the line, divided by the minutes left.
  • Above pace means the match is adding corners faster than the line requires. Below pace is the reverse.
  • Pace is shown from the first minute but labelled early before the 15th. No projection is shown until then, or when the provider has not published corner counts. An absent count is not a zero.
